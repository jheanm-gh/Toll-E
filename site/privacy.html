Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Toll-E app for Android

Toll-E is built to need as little information about you as possible. You do not need an account, and Toll-E has no analytics SDK. This policy explains what information is handled, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who is responsible

The responsible party for Toll-E is Jhean-Michael van der Merwe, South Africa, who is also the Information Officer. Contact: support.tolle@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Your default vehicle class and settings, including whether personalised ads are on.
  • Favourite plazas, saved and recent calculations, and recently viewed plazas. You can clear your history or delete all of these in More → Privacy.
  • A copy of the toll data, so the app works offline.
  • Whether you have removed ads (only in versions that offer ad removal).
  • A random code created by the app, used only to limit spam in correction reports.

This information is not sent to Toll-E. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

3. Your location

Toll-E asks for location permission only when you tap “Near me” or “Use my location”. Your location is read once, on your device, to sort toll plazas by distance. It is not stored and is not sent to Toll-E or anyone else. Toll-E never uses location in the background.

4. Information that leaves your device

What Who receives it Why
Requests for toll data, which, like any internet request, reveal your IP address The Toll-E server, hosted by Supabase, Inc. To download current toll prices
Map image requests, which reveal your IP address and the area of the map you view OpenFreeMap, the map tile provider To draw the map
Correction reports you choose to send: the kind of problem, your message, the plaza concerned, an optional email address, the random device code, and the app and data versions The Toll-E server (Supabase), or your email app if you report by email To check and fix toll information, and to reply if you gave an email address
The plaza's position, when you ask for directions The navigation app you choose (for example Google Maps or Waze) To open directions; that app's own privacy policy applies
Advertising data (see section 5) Google (AdMob) To show and measure ads in the free version
Purchase data (see section 6), only in versions that offer ad removal Google Play and RevenueCat To process and restore the ad-removal purchase

5. Advertising

The free version shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit fraud and measure performance, Google receives information such as your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and the device advertising ID. Toll-E asks Google for non-personalised ads unless you switch on “Personalised ads” in More → Privacy. Where the law requires consent, for example in the EU or UK, Google's consent message is shown first, and you can change your choice later in More → Privacy.

Google's use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ads are never shown on the map, in the calculator or next to directions. If you remove ads, the advertising SDK is not started at all.

6. Purchases

Where Toll-E offers the once-off ad-removal purchase, payment is handled by Google Play. Toll-E never sees your card details. RevenueCat, Inc., the purchase service Toll-E uses, receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt. This lets it confirm the purchase and restore it on a new device. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

7. What Toll-E does not do

  • No accounts, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.
  • No collection of contacts, photos, files or location history.

8. Information sent outside South Africa

Google, Supabase, RevenueCat and OpenFreeMap may process information on servers outside South Africa. Toll-E uses these providers because they are bound by data-protection terms that give protection comparable to POPIA (section 72).

9. How long information is kept

  • Correction reports and any email address you included are deleted within 12 months of the report being resolved.
  • Server request logs kept by the hosting provider are kept for that provider's standard log period.
  • Information on your device stays until you delete it (More → Privacy → Clear history or Delete all saved items) or uninstall the app.

10. Security

All connections from the app use encryption in transit (HTTPS). The toll database allows the public to read only published toll data. Correction reports are writable but not readable by the public.

11. Children

Toll-E is a tool for road users and is not directed at children under 13.

12. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy. You may ask us to correct or delete it, and you may object to its processing. Email support.tolle@gmail.com. To find a report you sent, we may ask for the date and plaza it concerned, because Toll-E has no accounts.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Toll-E is independent and is not affiliated with SANRAL or any toll operator.
