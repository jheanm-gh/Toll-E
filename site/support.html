Support

Email support.tolle@gmail.com. Toll-E is maintained by one person, so allow a few working days for a reply.

Report a wrong price or plaza

In the app, open the plaza and tap Report incorrect information, or go to More → Report incorrect information. Include what you saw at the plaza (amount, vehicle class and date) and, if you can, a link to the operator's notice. Reports are checked against official sources before anything changes.

Common questions

Why does a price say “Unverified” or “Under review”?

Toll-E shows every price with its source. “Unverified” means it was taken from a published source but a person has not yet checked it against the Government Gazette or the operator's notice. “Under review” means sources disagree or an automatic check flagged the value. Treat both as indicative and confirm at the plaza.

When do toll prices change?

Most national toll tariffs change on 1 March each year. Toll-E updates its data online, without an app update, and shows new prices as “upcoming” before they take effect.

Does Toll-E work offline?

Yes. It uses the last toll data it downloaded and tells you when that was.

How do I restore ad removal on a new phone?

Where ad removal is offered, sign in to the same Google Play account and tap More → Restore purchase.

Your data

Toll-E keeps your settings and saved trips only on your phone; uninstalling the app deletes them. To ask about or delete a correction report you sent, email us with the date and the plaza it concerned. See the privacy policy.

Toll-E is independent and is not affiliated with SANRAL or any toll operator.
