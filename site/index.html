Toll-E

Know your tolls. Know your costs. Know before you go.

Toll-E is a free Android app for South African road users. It shows toll plaza prices for every vehicle class, the plazas on a map, and what a trip will cost one way or return.

What it does

  • A directory of South African toll plazas, searchable by name, road and town.
  • Prices for Class 1 to Class 4 vehicles, with the tariff period, the source and whether the price has been verified.
  • A toll calculator that lists every plaza on the trip, handles ramp plazas you may or may not pass, and says plainly when a total is partial or an estimate.
  • Works offline with the last downloaded data. No account needed.

Where the prices come from

Prices come from the Government Gazette, SANRAL and the toll concessionaires' published tariffs. Toll-E shows the source for every price. Prices that a person has not yet checked against an official source are labelled. Always confirm the amount displayed at the plaza.
