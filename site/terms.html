1. Information, not advice

Toll-E provides information about South African toll plazas and toll costs, gathered from official and other published sources. Each price shows its source and whether a person has verified it. Trip totals are estimates based on the plazas listed. Your actual route, how the operator classifies your vehicle, discounts, frequent-user schemes and operator decisions can change what you pay. Partial totals and unverified prices are clearly marked.
